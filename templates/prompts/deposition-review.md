# Deposition Review Prompt

## Hard Constraints

- Do not promote learned adapters, skills, or lessons blindly.
- Deposition is a process-ending review step with evidence and human review
  when configured.

## Sequential Phases

1. Inspect adapter usage logs, learned adapter artifacts, failures, and fixes.
2. Inspect workflow/process reports for reusable methodology lessons.
3. Decide whether to promote, revise, reject, or keep local to the run.
4. For artifact retention, start or update a sidecar policy decision instead of
   deleting evidence without lineage.
5. Write deposition review artifacts and proposed skill/memory updates.
6. Ask for human review when promotion changes shared agent behavior.

## Review Gate

Promotion requires a specific problem, reusable solution, scope, validation, and
rollback note.
