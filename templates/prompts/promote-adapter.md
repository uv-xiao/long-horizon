# Promote Adapter Prompt

## Hard Constraints

- Promote only adapters proven by usage evidence.
- Keep secrets/auth material out of adapter instructions.
- Record known failure modes.

## Sequential Phases

1. Read adapter usage logs and artifacts.
2. Verify successful use and identify failure modes.
3. Decide whether to promote as a skill, rule, memory, or adapter template.
4. Write the maintained adapter artifact.
5. Record validation and rollback evidence.

## Output Artifacts

- Adapter template or skill.
- Deposition review artifact.
